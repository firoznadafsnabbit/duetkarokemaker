01 Overview & Privacy Policy

Welcome to Karoke Maker, operated by Ayesha Tech Studio ("we", "us", "our"). We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is kept secure.

🔒 Privacy First: Your audio files and song lyrics are processed privately on your own device. We do not store, listen to, or share your music files on external servers.

This Privacy Policy explains what basic information we collect, why we need it, how we protect it, and your rights.

02 Information We Collect

We believe in strict data privacy. We only collect the minimal details needed to manage your user account:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we securely store your email address and encrypted login credentials.
  • Token & Package Balance: We maintain your active token balance to allow you to create and download your videos.
  • Security Logs: Standard web security records (such as general IP location and device type) used solely to prevent spam and protect account safety.

03 Private & Secure Video Creation

Karoke Maker is built to prioritize user privacy, data security, and instant performance:

  • Your music tracks stay safely on your own computer or mobile phone.
  • Karaoke lyrics, timing, and duet color highlights are generated smoothly and privately.
  • Your completed 1080p MP4 karaoke video downloads straight to your device without unnecessary waiting.

04 Essential Cookies & Storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We only use essential local storage to keep your session working:

Storage Item Type Purpose
Account Session Secure Storage Keeps you safely signed into your account between visits.
User Preferences Local Storage Remembers your display settings and cookie preferences.

05 Payment Information Security

Payments for token packages and software licenses are completed via direct Unified Payments Interface (UPI) banking apps:

  • We never request, view, or store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, or net banking passwords.
  • UPI payments are securely handled within your authorized banking app (e.g. Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).

06 Legal Compliance (DPDP, GDPR & CCPA)

We respect consumer privacy standards and comply with applicable data protection laws:

  • India DPDP Act: You have full rights to review your account details, correct information, and request account deletion.
  • GDPR & International Standards: You have the right to access, rectify, or request permanent deletion of your account at any time.
  • Advertising Privacy: We do not sell or share personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Age Requirement: Our service is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

07 Account & Data Deletion Rights

You have complete control over your account. You can request permanent deletion of your profile, email, and tokens at any time:

  • Use the in-app deletion button at the bottom of the home screen, or
  • Email our support desk at feroznadafm@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Upon verification, your account records and profile data will be permanently deleted within 48 hours.
