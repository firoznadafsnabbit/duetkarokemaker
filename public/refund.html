01 Digital Nature of Services

Karoke Maker delivers digital video generation credits ("tokens"). Because our digital items are credited immediately upon payment confirmation, they do not involve physical goods delivery or shipping.

🛡️ Our Customer Guarantee: If any technical rendering error prevents you from downloading your video after tokens were deducted, we will immediately restore your tokens or issue a full refund upon verification.

02 Eligibility for Refund

You are eligible for a cash refund under the following circumstances:

  • Technical Failure: If a server or engine malfunction prevented video rendering, and our support team is unable to resolve the issue within 24 hours of notification.
  • Duplicate Charges: If you were accidentally charged twice for the same token top-up order via UPI or payment link.
  • Non-Delivery of Tokens: If your UPI transaction was successful and debited from your bank account, but your account balance was not credited within 2 hours.

03 Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds cannot be issued under the following conditions:

  • Videos that have already been rendered and successfully downloaded to your device.
  • User errors in typing lyrics, misspelling singer tags, or uploading incorrect audio tracks (our real-time preview player allows full verification before clicking "Render Video").
  • Purchased tokens that remain unused in your account (tokens have no expiration date and remain valid indefinitely for future videos).

04 Instant Free Token Re-Credit Guarantee

If you experience any technical disruption (such as a temporary device glitch or network disconnection during download), our support team can immediately top up your account with replacement tokens for free within minutes, without having to wait for bank refund cycles.
